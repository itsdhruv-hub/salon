Package Guidelines

Find quick answers regarding our luxury pricing packages, consultations, and payment guidelines.

Are the packages customizable?

Yes, absolutely. If you wish to swap a treatment in a package (e.g. swap a Gentlemen's Facial for a Head Massage), simply mention it to your concierge at check-in or state it in your reservation message.

Which payment modes do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, and secure bank transfers for bridal events.
